Roles

I serve with International Media Ministries, which creates Bible-based films and shares them with partners around the world in more than 20 languages. Here’s what I do there.

Distribution Coordinator

I connect partners around the world with the films and content we create here at IMM.

IT Manager

Servers, cloud, CPUs, mice, keyboards and everything in between: if it’s broken, I try to fix it, future-proof it, or simply make it work.

Resident Chef

As part of the Logistics and Hospitality team, I cook for our guests. It draws on everything I’ve learned since I was five: helping my mom, an international chef, then serving as her sous chef in my teens, working at Chick-fil-A, running my own pop-up restaurant, and culinary school. Now all of it gets brought to bear in service of the Kingdom.

Privacy

This site belongs to Sean Mills. Here’s exactly what happens when you press “Pray for Sean.”

What’s collected

The date and time you pressed the button, whether you came from a prayer card or the website, and your name and email only if you choose to enter them.

Why

Your name lets me know who’s praying. Your email is used only to send my newsletter, Tilting at windMills.

Where it’s kept

In a private Google Sheet that only I can access. It’s never sold or shared.

Security check

The button uses Cloudflare Turnstile to block spam bots. It doesn’t use advertising cookies.

Your choices

Reply “unsubscribe” to any newsletter to stop receiving it. To see or delete what I hold about you, email sean@millsonmission.com. These rights include those under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
